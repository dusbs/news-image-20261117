## What is in here

- `main.py` — the entry program (Python 3.13, standard library only). It copies `entry.png` into the output directory, byte for byte, every time.
- `entry.png` — the front page itself, 1024&times;1024.
- `design.html` — the design source the PNG was rendered from.

## The stories

- **Lead:** Iran strikes the US radar in Bahrain; US jets answer on Qeshm Island after a tanker is mined in the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Markets:** stocks plunge, the Nasdaq slips into correction, Brent crude falls to $92.44.
- **Ukraine:** a 1,300-drone barrage hits the Crimea bridge; Russia strikes two bridges in Kyiv.
- **Cyberattack:** malicious code slipped in via Optimizely reaches 178 sites, including BBC, CNN, Zoom and HSBC.
- **Japan:** fighter jets scramble as Russian aircraft enter its airspace.
- **Paris:** Depardieu drops his appeal and admits a sexual assault charge.
